Post idea: is IndieWeb the old internet I have been searching for?

Most projects that try to revive the old internet focus on nostalgia alone, which is fine for 10 minutes but then you discover
that the world has moved on and things simply cannot work like they did back in the 00's

What we are looking for is the freedom and sense of community, not necessarily the way everything worked
IndieWeb and Fediverse seem to be the answer. Those are modern tools, based on how web works right now, not how it used to work 25 years ago
And they provided a larger sense of community and freedom
